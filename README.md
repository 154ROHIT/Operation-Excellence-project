
# Manufacturing Process Study & Improvement Opportunities

> **Shop-floor observations, structured data, and visual tools to understand manufacturing flow.**

This project documents manufacturing process studies carried out at **SKH Manesar and SKH Pune**. The work focused on understanding operations, measuring activity times, visualizing material and trolley movement, and identifying areas for further improvement.

## Project Scope

- **Process mapping** across manufacturing areas and production stages
- **Time studies** of die changeovers and production activities
- **Paint shop study** of unloading activities, wagon movement, and final quality inspection
- **VA/NVA review** to distinguish value-added from non-value-added work
- **Operational analysis** of delays, bottlenecks, OEE, rework, and rejection trends
- **Visual simulation** of trolley and basket movement through the process

## Approach

```text
Observe → Record → Map → Analyze → Visualize → Identify opportunities
```

Shop-floor observations and activity times were organized using Excel and Gantt charts. Process and layout maps helped make workflows and movement easier to understand. A Python simulation was developed with AI-assisted coding to visualize wagon movement and demonstrate how delays can affect flow.

## Tools & Methods

- **Excel** — data organization and activity-time records
- **Gantt charts** — task sequence and duration visualization
- **Process and layout mapping** — workflow and trolley-flow visualization
- **Python** — process simulation
- **AI-assisted coding** — support during simulation code development
- **Time study, VA/NVA review, root-cause and OEE analysis** — study methods

## Key Outcomes

- Documented manufacturing workflows and shop-floor activities.
- Identified VA/NVA activities and recorded alarm-related delays affecting wagon movement.
- Created visual materials to communicate task timing, layout, and process flow.
- Highlighted areas for follow-up on productivity, material flow, bottlenecks, and quality.

The studies provide a fact base for improvement discussions. **No quantified operational gains are claimed here.**

## Learning & Development

The project strengthened practical skills in shop-floor observation, structured data collection, process visualization, and communicating operational findings. It also provided experience using digital tools to make manufacturing processes easier to explain.
